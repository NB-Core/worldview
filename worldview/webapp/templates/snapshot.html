Quadrant guide

Every field card sits in one of four boxes — based on its Valuation Score (cheap → expensive) and Sentiment Score (fearful → greedy). The action label is shorthand for the typical entry posture:

BUY val ≤ 50 · sent ≤ 50

Cheap on fundamentals and fearful on sentiment — Buffett's preferred entry. Mean-reversion has both factors working with you.

CAUTION val ≤ 50 · sent ≥ 50

Cheap on paper but the crowd is already running hot. Late-cycle pile-on risk — the trade may still lift off, but sentiment is no tailwind. Wait for a dip.

TRAP val ≥ 50 · sent ≤ 50

Expensive and nobody likes it. Cheap-looking screens lie — the market's pricing in trouble, and momentum is gone. Often grinds lower for months.

BUBBLE val ≥ 50 · sent ≥ 50

Expensive and euphoric. Classic late-cycle warning. Reduce, hedge, or stay clear; the asymmetry has flipped against new buyers.
