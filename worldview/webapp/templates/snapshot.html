Regime guide · model v2

Valuation measures fundamental multiples; Sentiment runs from fear to greed; Macro risk runs from low stress to high stress. Scores describe historical ranks, not forecasts.

45–55 on either regime axis is TRANSITION. Outside this band: BUY = low valuation / weak sentiment (legacy name, no buy recommendation); CAUTION = low valuation / strong sentiment; TRAP = high valuation / weak sentiment (no fundamental Value Trap diagnosis); BUBBLE = high valuation / strong sentiment (no proof of a speculative bubble).

Price / Momentum is a historical price-level rank, not a return momentum measure. Missing fundamental histories remain N/A. Legacy quadrants retain the 50 threshold.
